There is a point on the Madhyamaheshwar trail where the path turns and the
valley simply opens, and Chaukhamba is no longer something on a map but a wall
of snow standing across the morning. I had seen the name for years. I was not
ready for the size of it.

What surprised me was how little I thought once I was there. I had carried a
whole argument up the hill with me — about work, about what I was doing with my
life — and the mountain took it without a word. Not solved. Just quieted, the
way a loud room goes still when something larger walks in.

## What the photograph leaves out

A picture flattens it. The cold that came off the snow even in sunlight, the
thinness of the air that made me stop more than my pride wanted to, the sound of
nothing — none of that survives the frame. I am keeping the photograph anyway,
because it is the closest I can hand you. But the real thing was mostly silence,
and you cannot crop silence into an image.

I came down slower than I went up. That is usually how it goes.
